about=COGO Workbench brings an ArcGIS Pro style course-entry workflow to QGIS. Paste a metes-and-bounds legal description and review every extracted call against its source clause before loading, or enter bearings, distances and circular curves by hand. Optional deed reading sends page images to a local Ollama model, or to xAI (Grok) at https://api.x.ai when you supply XAI_API_KEY or a QGIS authentication config. Nothing is drawn until you review the calls. The plugin reports misclosure and precision, can apply a Bowditch (compass rule) adjustment, and writes boundary lines or parcel polygons with true circular arcs. Calculations are planar in a projected CRS with explicit ground-to-grid scale and rotation settings. Pure Python: QGIS, its bundled Qt and the standard library only. No extra packages. Independent of Esri.
